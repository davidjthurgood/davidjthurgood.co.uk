04 — Santa Fe & Taos

Santa Fe & Taos

27 Jun – 1 Jul

Explore

  • Meow Wolf — House of Eternal Return
    Spend a few hours exploring Meow Wolf Santa Fe's House of Eternal Return, Santa Fe's famous immersive art experience. It's worth booking tickets in advance and selecting a timed entry slot, especially during weekends and busy travel periods.
  • Saturday Morning Farmers Market
    Visit the Santa Fe Farmers Market on Saturday morning in the Railyard District. It's one of the best places to experience local New Mexico produce, food vendors, baked goods, and the atmosphere of Santa Fe's creative community. Arrive early for breakfast and to browse before the crowds build.
  • Bandelier National Monument
    Bandelier National Monument is one of northern New Mexico's most fascinating historical and archaeological sites. Follow the Main Loop Trail to see ancient cliff dwellings and cave-like homes carved into the soft volcanic tuff by the Ancestral Pueblo people. Ladders allow visitors to climb into several of the dwellings, while the surrounding canyon offers beautiful hiking and impressive scenery. Plan to spend at least half a day here to fully appreciate both the history and the landscape. The drive from Santa Fe to Bandelier National Monument is an attraction in itself — the route winds through the forested hills of the Pajarito Plateau, offering expansive views of mesas, canyons, and volcanic landscapes.
  • Valles Caldera National Preserve
    Spend time exploring Valles Caldera, a vast volcanic basin created by a massive eruption over a million years ago. The preserve is known for its wide-open grasslands, mountain views, wildlife, and scenic drives. Keep an eye out for elk, especially in the early morning or evening. The landscape feels more like Montana than the desert Southwest and offers some of the most spectacular natural scenery in northern New Mexico.
  • Walatowa Slot Canyon & the Red Rocks of Jemez
    Take a day trip into the Jemez Mountains to visit Walatowa Slot Canyon, a beautiful narrow canyon carved through striking red sandstone. The short walk through the canyon feels surprisingly remote and offers a completely different landscape from the high desert around Santa Fe. Nearby, the dramatic red rock formations around Jemez Pueblo make for excellent scenery and photography.
  • The High Road to Taos
    Rather than the direct route, drive the High Road to Taos. This winding scenic road passes adobe churches, mountain villages, art studios, and incredible views of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains. Consider taking the High Road one way and the Low Road through the Rio Grande canyon back for a different perspective of northern New Mexico.
  • The Turquoise Trail (Santa Fe → Albuquerque)
    Take the scenic Turquoise Trail between Santa Fe and Albuquerque. The route passes through historic mining towns and some beautiful high-desert landscapes. Make a stop in Madrid, a quirky former mining town filled with galleries, shops, and cafés. While there, grab a green chile cheeseburger at the legendary Mineshaft Tavern.
  • Horseback Riding — Rio Grande Stables
    Look into a trail ride with Rio Grande Stables. Their Cebolla Mesa rides offer a chance to explore the landscapes around the Rio Grande Gorge on horseback and experience a quieter side of northern New Mexico.

Eat

    New Mexican Classics
  • Local #1 pick
    La Choza
    4.6 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Chile Relleno plate, Pork Adovada Burrito Grande, fish tacos, Frito Pie — all "Christmas" (red + green chile). Don't miss the mocha cake dessert.
    Voted #1 NM restaurant by Santa Fe Reporter readers. Sister to The Shed — locals consistently prefer this one for its relaxed vibe, sopaipillas, and better parking. No dinner reservations; arrive early or wait with a margarita. Closed Sundays.
  • James Beard America's Classic
    The Shed
    4.3 TripAdvisor3.5 Yelp
    Order: Red chile enchiladas (blue corn), carne adovada, green chile stew. Note: no sopaipillas here.
    Same Carswell family as La Choza, open since 1953. Set in a stunning 1692 adobe hacienda near the Plaza — they grind red chile fresh daily on-site. More touristy than La Choza but genuinely charming. Reservations recommended for dinner.
  • Breakfast & lunch only · closes 2pm
    Tia Sophia's
    4.4 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Breakfast burrito or Huevos Rancheros — both Christmas style (red + green). A Santa Fe rite of passage.
    Hole-in-the-wall diner just off the Plaza, beloved by locals for 40+ years. Fast, friendly, no-frills. Closes at 2pm sharp — plan your morning around it. Cash-friendly.
    • Fine Dining & Special Occasions
  • James Beard Best Chef SW 2022
    Sazón
    4.6 Google4.5 Yelp
    Order: Duck with mole, mole tasting flight, shrimp soup. Chef Olea specialises in complex moles — let him guide you. Ask for the mole sampler.
    Chef Fernando Olea won James Beard Best Chef Southwest in 2022. Gorgeous murals, warm atmosphere, Latin American fine dining far beyond the standard. Book well ahead. Around $60–100+ per head. One of the best meals you'll have in New Mexico.
  • James Beard America's Classic 1999
    Café Pasqual's
    4.4 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Huevos Motuleños (eggs on corn tortilla with black beans, banana, feta, peas, salsa), mole-rolled enchiladas, corn pancakes. Breakfast is legendary.
    Founded 1979, a Santa Fe icon. Colourful papel picado, folk art paintings, communal centre table. Rated #1 in Santa Fe by Tripexpert (Frommer's, Travel + Leisure, Fodor's all endorse it). Often a wait — go after 1:30pm or join the communal table. Open for all meals.
  • Paloma
    4.5 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Chicken en mole, tuna tostaditos (avocado, chipotle mayo, salsa macha, serranos, crispy scallions).
    The Infatuation's pick for "a big night out with great food and drinks." Energetic, good cocktails, lively atmosphere. Great for a dinner where you want vibes as much as the food. Reservation recommended.
    • Steakhouse & BBQ
  • Fine diningJames Beard featured chef
    Market Steer Steakhouse
    4.7 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Any prime cut + green chile risotto side (non-negotiable), bone marrow, Tex-Mex mussels to start. Try the NM cut with Crab Oscar.
    Chef Kathleen Crook — Artesia native, James Beard Taste America featured chef — puts a New Mexico spin on prime steaks. Steps from the Plaza at Hotel St. Francis. Dinner only Tue–Sat plus weekend brunch. Reservation essential. $100+ per head but worth every cent for a special occasion.
  • Dinner only · Thu–Sat
    Steam & Smoke Craft BBQ
    4.4 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Brisket, smoked duck, green chile cornbread, ribs.
    Top-rated BBQ in Santa Fe. Run by a chef with serious technique — elevated craft BBQ with bold local flavours at the Railyard. Very limited hours (Thu–Sat evenings), newer spot. Call ahead to confirm hours before going.
    • Casual, Street Food & Tacos
  • Food truckJames Beard semifinalist
    El Chile Toreado
    4.8 Google4.5 Yelp
    Order: "Luis Mix" breakfast burrito (chorizo, bacon, Polish sausage, eggs, potatoes, green chile — massive), al pastor tacos, adobada tacos, carnitas. House-made green chile sauce is essential.
    A true Santa Fe institution. Run by Luis & Berenice Medina — he's a decades-long hot dog cart legend, she has a Le Cordon Bleu degree. Paper plates, outdoor seating, a buzzer system when your food's ready. Open Mon–Sat from 7:30am, closes 2:30pm (Sat 2pm). Small card surcharge. Go before noon for best selection.
  • Tune-Up Café
    4.4 Google4.4 TripAdvisor
    Order: Pupusas (Salvadoran cult classic), smoked duck hash, lamb barbacoa taco, mole chicken enchiladas, Huevos El Salvadoreños. Check the specials board.
    Chef Jesus Rivera trained at Café Pasqual's. Friendly, funky neighbourhood spot a little off the tourist trail — which is exactly what makes it special. Breakfast through dinner daily, backroom bar with craft beers on tap, great patio. Repeat visitors swear by it.
    • Historic & Casual
  • The Pink Adobe & Dragon Room Bar
    4.2 Google3.5 Yelp
    Order: Steak Dunigan (green chile), French apple pie. Or just come for cocktails in the Dragon Room.
    Great vibe and old-Santa Fe atmosphere — the Dragon Room bar out front is a beloved local institution in its own right. The restaurant leans more on atmosphere than cutting-edge food. Come for the scene and the history, not the cuisine.
  • Plaza Café
    4.3 Google3.5 Yelp
    Order: Green chile cheeseburger, blue plate daily specials, breakfast classics.
    A Santa Fe diner institution since 1947, right on the Plaza. Reliable comfort food, fast service, no pretension. Perfect for a casual lunch between sightseeing. Not cutting-edge, but solidly good and supremely convenient.
    • Dessert & Treats
  • Kakawa Chocolate House
    4.7 Google4.5 Yelp
    Order: Hot chocolate elixir flight, Goat Cheese & Sage truffle, Red Chile Caramel, Piñon Caramel, blue corn chili brownie ice cream sundae. They even have a 1790s Jeffersonian elixir based on Thomas Jefferson's personal recipe.
    Mandatory afternoon stop. Handcrafted chocolates using organic, fair-trade, small-batch cacao. Extremely knowledgeable staff. One of the few places open until 6pm. A professional chef described it as a "bucket list" experience. Go before your flight home.
    • On the Turquoise Trail
  • Mineshaft Tavern, Madrid
    Order: Green chile cheeseburger — the reason to stop.
    A legendary stop on the Turquoise Trail in the quirky former mining town of Madrid. Best known for its green chile cheeseburger — grab one while you're browsing the town's galleries and shops.

Drink

    Rooftop & Views
  • Must-do sunset spot
    Bell Tower Bar — La Fonda on the Plaza
    4.4 Google3.5 Yelp
    Order: Margarita at golden hour — the views are the main event.
    Head up to the Bell Tower Bar at La Fonda on the Plaza for sunset. The rooftop views over Santa Fe and the surrounding mountains are supposed to be some of the best in town, making it a perfect spot for an evening margarita. Arrive a little before sunset to get a good table. Seasonal — may close in winter months.
  • Best rooftop bar
    Coyote Café Rooftop Cantina
    4.3 Google3.5 Yelp
    Order: Norteño margarita, seasonal cocktails. Good bar snacks too.
    Quintessential Santa Fe experience. Open-air rooftop above Water Street — brightly coloured tables, street-art murals, great people-watching. Buzzy, energetic, fun. Seasonal (check it's open). The ground-floor Coyote Café restaurant is a totally different, more refined experience.
  • La Posada Courtyard
    4.5 Google
    Order: Margarita or wine in the landscaped courtyard garden.
    The courtyard at La Posada de Santa Fe resort is genuinely beautiful — lush gardens, relaxed atmosphere, far quieter than the rooftop bar scene. A more elegant, unhurried option for an afternoon or pre-dinner drink. Great vibe.
    • Cocktail Bars
  • Yelp #1 cocktail bar
    Secreto Lounge
    4.5 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Sage-smoked margarita, Spicy Secreto, Agave Way (green chile infused), any seasonal "garden-to-glass" cocktail. Happy hour deals available.
    Santa Fe's premier craft cocktail bar. Award-winning "garden-to-glass" philosophy — fresh local herbs, house-made syrups, NM distillery spirits. Intimate, candlelit, sophisticated. Lovely front veranda for people-watching. Sit at the bar for guaranteed good service. Also at Hotel St. Francis — easy to combine with Market Steer for a full evening.
  • Milk of the Poppy
    4.5 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Trust the bartender — ask for a recommendation based on your mood. Creative and inventive menu.
    Makes Yelp's top 10 cocktail bars. NYC visitors call it "hands down the best cocktails in Santa Fe." Great for a late evening when you want something more adventurous than a standard margarita. Edgy, imaginative, fun.
  • La Plazuela / Julia / Agave — La Fonda
    4.3 Google
    Order: Specialty margaritas, house cocktails, local spirits.
    Three connected bar options within or around La Fonda Hotel on the Plaza — a beautiful historic property. La Plazuela is the indoor courtyard restaurant bar with stunning architecture. Good for a pre-dinner drink in an elegant setting.
    • Local & Dive Bars
  • Best margaritas · best value
    Del Charro
    4.3 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: House margarita — notoriously potent, famously affordable. Bar snacks and Southwestern bites.
    True local favourite. Dark wood interior, welcoming fireplace, consistently buzzing crowd. The house margaritas are a Santa Fe legend for strength-to-price ratio. One of the top insider picks and consistently mentioned alongside Secreto as a must-do bar stop.
  • Yelp #1 dive bar
    The Matador
    4.3 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Cold beer, whatever's on tap. No frills required.
    Grungy, punk, unpretentious — the antidote to Santa Fe's polished cocktail bar scene. Top-rated dive bar on Yelp. A genuine local institution for people who like their bars honest and their drinks cheap. Great for a late-night wind-down.
  • Evangelo's
    4.2 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Classic drinks, pool table. Just hang out.
    Long-running downtown bar with a consistent local crowd. Makes Yelp's top 10 unique bars. Pool table, no pretension, good energy. A Santa Fe drinking staple that's been going for decades.
  • Joe's Tequila Bar
    4.3 Google3.5 Yelp
    Order: Tequila flight, house margarita, mezcal cocktail.
    Exactly what it says — a dedicated tequila bar downtown. Good selection of agave spirits, lively atmosphere, popular with a mix of tourists and locals. A solid stop if you want to explore beyond the standard margarita.
    • Breweries
  • Tumbleroot Brewery & Distillery
    4.4 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: Local craft beer or house spirits. Check their live music schedule before going.
    Lively, fun, local favourite with regular live music. Their sister venue Tumbleroot Pottery Pub (top 10 unique bars on Yelp) lets you throw clay on a pottery wheel while drinking a craft cocktail — genuinely one of the most Santa Fe things you can do.
  • Yelp top 10 unique bars
    Tumbleroot Pottery Pub
    4.4 Google4.0 Yelp
    Order: A handmade craft cocktail while you make something on the pottery wheel.
    Top 10 unique bars on Yelp Santa Fe. Throw clay, drink cocktails, meet locals — completely unique and very on-brand for Santa Fe's artsy scene. Book pottery sessions in advance if possible. Genuinely fun for any group.
  • Nuckolls Brewing
    4.4 Google
    Order: Seasonal house brews — ask what's freshest on tap.
    Solid local craft brewery. Good option if you want a more laid-back beer-focused evening without the cocktail scene energy. Welcoming taproom atmosphere.
